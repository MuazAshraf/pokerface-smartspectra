Before we begin

Entertainment game. Not a real lie detector or medical tool.

  • Your camera is used to estimate pulse, breathing and facial expression. The Presage SmartSpectra SDK does this on your computer; Presage's server is only contacted to validate the app's API key.
  • Your answers and a short summary of signal changes (e.g. "pulse +8 BPM") are sent to OpenAI to write the detective's questions. No video or images leave your computer.
  • If you answer out loud, your microphone audio is streamed to Deepgram for transcription only while you are answering, and the detective's voice is generated by Deepgram (opted out of Deepgram's model-improvement program). You can always type instead.
  • Nothing is saved. Everything is discarded when the game ends.
